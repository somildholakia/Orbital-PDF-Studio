Privacy policy

A plain-language privacy note for Orbital PDF Studio and its browser-based PDF processing.

Open the PDF workspace ↗

PDF files

The studio uses browser-side code to read your selected files and create exports. It does not upload the PDF contents to an Orbital PDF Studio application server.

External resources

PDF processing libraries are loaded from public CDN providers. Your browser makes normal requests to those providers to download the code needed to use this site.

Local preferences

Theme and accent preferences may be saved in your browser’s local storage on this device. They are not used as a server-side profile.

Limits

Do not use the studio as a substitute for secure document retention or legal advice. You are responsible for reviewing exported files before sharing them.
